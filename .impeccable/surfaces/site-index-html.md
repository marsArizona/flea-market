# Surface: Bastians Flea Market — home (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).
Audience: buyers in Mexico, mostly on phones from a shared link. Job: see every item, price and condition, then contact the seller. Action: "Apartar por WhatsApp" (wa.me/525588099786, prefilled) or the message form (Web3Forms → owner's email).
Constraints: products visible immediately; no cart; no invented claims (no shipping, warranty, reviews).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a provincial dagashi-ya seen straight on: a noren over the door, then shelves where each item sits on a rail and its hand-written price strip hangs off the shelf edge. It refuses the e-commerce grid of equal rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Warm aluminium grey ground (TE body), black noren and shelf rails, white price strips lettered in orange marker (Yusei Magic), heavy Dela Gothic One signage, vertical single-kanji shelf-end signs (弾 作 録 聴; the okurigana く in Dela Gothic One reads as a "<" arrow at sign scale, so it was dropped), orange hanko 売約済 for sold items. Real, uncropped photos of each unit. On phones each shelf stacks two items per board; nothing scrolls sideways.

STORY: The visitor scans every shelf, reads prices at a glance, taps an item; a drawer slides out under that shelf with photos, 5-row spec sheet, condition and blurb; they message by WhatsApp or the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width black noren (cloth panels with slits) carrying "Bastians Flea Market" + 蚤の市, one line on how buying works, then the first shelf (弾 / para tocar) with three items and their tags. Primary action: each item opens its drawer whose first button is WhatsApp.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "que el sitio tenga los colores y distribucion como si fuera esas tiendas japonesas de curiosidades o dulces en provincia japonesa, ademas de la paleta de tenage enngenering que es como gris con naranja y blanco y negro ... el mainsite pues directamente que muestre los productos". Signature interaction: the drawer pulls out of the shelf; tags swing on hover. Motion grammar: one noren sway on load, drawer slide with exponential ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
